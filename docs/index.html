How does AI-generated mathematics cite the literature?

Citation behaviour in the 722 preprints OpenAI released in openai/math (September–October 2026), compared with a subject-matched random sample of – human-written arXiv papers whose first version was posted in 2020–2022.

The AI papers cite the literature like a toolbox: they usually name the exact theorem or lemma they use, cite one work at a time, and cite inside proofs about twice as often as human authors.

The AI corpus on its own

Two questions that need no human baseline: do the AI papers cite each other, and do their DOIs point to the works they claim?

DOI checks compare each cited DOI with its Crossref or DataCite record. Flagged cases include legitimate differences (a chapter cited under its book's DOI, translated titles) and are listed for manual review; they are not an error rate.
